How it started

The Moment I Knew This Was My Work

I went to a women's entrepreneur meetup in Valencia and offered to speak for free. I sat down with six different women. Looked at their businesses. Told them the truth. Handed each one a direction.

By the sixth conversation, I knew. This is what I should be doing. And I should be charging for it.

So I built Sage Owl Strategy — and The Sovereign Room, a Skool community with a low entry point, so no woman has to figure it out alone the way I did.

I'd spent years inside other people's businesses, making expensive mistakes of my own because I didn't have anyone to call. No one who'd answer a real question without making me feel like I owed them something. I didn't have a map. I was guessing. It cost me.

Now I run Sage Owl Strategy from my carry-on. Valencia. Bali. England. Wherever the map takes me. I bring the map to you.

What I Am. What I'm Not.

The Adult in the Room.

I am a business strategist. That is a very specific thing. I diagnose the positioning constraint capping your revenue. I hand you the precise, sequenced plan to fix it. Not a mindset journal. Not accountability calls. Not a $27 PDF with generic steps.

The word "coach" is an overused word in the online business world. It has been diluted beyond meaning. I don't use it. What I offer is a diagnostic and a plan. Built specifically for your business, in the exact order it needs to happen.

I help female founders identify why they're plateauing. That requires seeing what they can't see from the inside. That's not coaching. That's architecture.

My philosophy: Simplicity > complexity. The right answer in the right order beats a hundred good ideas going nowhere.
